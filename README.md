# Statistical Intelligence Academy

Statistical Intelligence Academy (SIA) is a gamified learning platform for Probability and Statistics designed to transform traditional statistics education into an engaging, mission-based learning experience.

The platform combines active learning, data-driven storytelling, interactive simulations, achievement systems, and real-world applications of probability, statistics, and data science. Students progress through academy ranks, complete missions and challenges, earn achievements, and develop the analytical thinking skills required in modern STEM disciplines.

## Vision

To create a modern educational environment where students learn Probability and Statistics through exploration, experimentation, and problem-solving rather than passive content consumption.

## Target Audience

* Computer Science students
* Computer Engineering students
* Electrical and Electronics Engineering students
* Information Technology students
* Mathematics students
* Data Science learners

## Core Features

* Mission-based learning
* Gamified progression system
* Experience points (XP) and achievements
* Interactive statistical simulations
* Probability and statistics visualizations
* Real-world data investigations
* Learning analytics and progress tracking
